# First-use study consent script

Read this text before starting. Do not paraphrase it.

> You are invited to test whether the Mnemox first-use experience is understandable and controllable. Participation is voluntary. You may skip a task or stop at any time without giving a reason.
>
> Use only the synthetic scenario supplied for this session. Do not enter or show names, email addresses, account details, prompts, responses, memories, credentials, personal information, source code, files, URLs, or other private content. The study will not record audio, video, or your screen.
>
> The facilitator records only participant code P01-P05, consent status and time, task outcomes, elapsed seconds, attempt counts, predefined intervention/severity codes, and sanitized issue codes. Your words and on-screen content will not be transcribed.
>
> Approved coded records are accessible only to the study owner and independent reviewer. Records are deleted 30 days after the Phase 0 exit decision. If you withdraw, your record is deleted within two business days. Aggregate findings that cannot be linked back to you may already have informed a decision.
>
> If prohibited information appears, the session stops and the material is quarantined and deleted under the incident procedure. The study is product research, not customer support or a promise of release.

Ask: "Do you voluntarily consent to continue under these conditions?"

Record only `accepted` or `declined` plus a UTC timestamp. A declined participant does not begin and receives no observation record beyond the declined consent entry, which is deleted within two business days.
